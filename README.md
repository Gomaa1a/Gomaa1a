## Ahmed Gomaa

**Data & AI engineer.** I turn messy ERP exports and spreadsheets into dashboards people actually make decisions with — and build AI products around them.

Based in Egypt. Most of my day-to-day work lives in private client repositories, so what's public here is a representative slice rather than the whole picture.

---

### What I do

**ERP & data pipelines** — Read-only query engines over Odoo databases, synced into Supabase, so reporting and dashboards never touch a live ERP.

**Analytics dashboards for operators** — Inventory, sales, and profitability dashboards built for the people making the call, not for analysts: frozen capital, dead vs. dormant stock, aging buckets, seasonality, salesperson performance.

**AI products** — Voice and conversational applications: live AI interview practice with CV-aware questions, text-to-speech, and scored feedback reports.

**Workflow automation** — n8n pipelines connecting CRMs, spreadsheets, and internal tools so data lands where it's needed without manual re-entry.

### Selected public work

**[Frozen Capital Dashboard](https://github.com/Gomaa1a/dabboos-frozen-capital)** — Splits a distributor's stuck inventory into *truly dead* and *seasonally dormant* capital. Aging buckets (60–90 / 90–180 / 180+), a live idle-threshold slider, per-category and per-brand breakdowns, and a monthly seasonality heatmap that flags what to clear now. Bilingual Arabic / English.

**[Retail Analytics Dashboard](https://github.com/Gomaa1a/E-commerce-Use-case-Data-Analytics-Dashboard-)** — A full year of beverage retail sales analysed from raw Excel: net profit, growth trends, best and worst performing products, and salesperson performance. Python (pandas) for the analysis, Chart.js for the interface.

**[HireReady](https://github.com/Gomaa1a/HireReady)** — An AI voice interview coach. It runs a live interview tailored to your CV, then returns an honest, scored performance report so each session is measurably better than the last. React, Supabase, OpenAI, ElevenLabs.

**[Law Firm SaaS](https://github.com/Gomaa1a/LawFirm-SAAS-Model)** — A demo SaaS model built for law firms.
